AI prompts for interview preparation

Interview practice needs a partner who asks questions and reacts to your answers. An AI assistant can play that part at any hour. It can also help you turn a rambling story into a clear answer. It cannot tell you what a particular company will ask, and its "typical questions" are general, so use it for practice and not for prediction.

The prompts

Run a mock interview

This works best as a conversation. The assistant asks, you answer, it responds.

Act as an interviewer for the role of [ROLE] at [TYPE OF EMPLOYER]. I have [YEARS] of experience.

Ask me one question at a time and wait for my answer. After each answer, give two lines of feedback: what worked and what to improve. Then ask the next question. Mix questions about my experience, my skills and how I handle situations. After six questions, give me an overall summary.

Begin with the first question.

Shape an answer about my experience

Help me answer this interview question: [THE QUESTION]

Here is what really happened: [TELL THE STORY IN YOUR OWN WORDS]

How to adapt these prompts

  • Name the role and the kind of employer. A bank, a school and a small trading firm ask different things.
  • Set the level. Add "I am a fresher" or "this is for a senior position" so that the questions fit.
  • Practise in the language of the interview. If it will be in Hindi or partly in Hindi, ask the assistant to run the mock interview that way.
  • Ask for harder questions. After one round, say "Now ask tougher follow-up questions on my weakest two answers".
  • Type your answers as you would speak them. If your assistant has a voice mode, speaking is better practice still.

Keep it honest and your own

Do not memorise the assistant's wording. Interviewers notice recited answers, and a follow-up question will expose them. Use the draft to get the order of your points right, then say it in your own words several times.

Remember that the questions an assistant suggests are general guesses. Read the advertisement, the employer's website and any official notice for the selection process yourself. For a government or bank recruitment, the official notification is the only reliable guide to what the stages are.
