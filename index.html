How to use this site

  1. Choose your task from the lists above, or type a word such as email, resume or quiz in the search box.
  2. Press "Copy prompt" under the prompt you want.
  3. Paste it into your AI assistant and replace each part in square brackets, such as [WHO THE READER IS], with your own details. The more real detail you give, the better the answer.
  4. Read the answer and reply with what to change: shorter, simpler, a different tone.
  5. Check the facts before you use it. The notes at the end of every task page say what to look for.

What makes these prompts different

Many prompt collections are long lists of one-line commands. The prompts here are built to make you supply the facts an assistant cannot know, and to stop it inventing what you did not say.

  • Placeholders for your facts. Each prompt shows exactly what to fill in.
  • Limits built in. Lines such as "Do not add facts that are not in my notes" and "If something is missing, ask me first" reduce made-up details.
  • Your work stays yours. Prompts for essays, resumes and interviews ask for feedback and coaching, not for a finished piece to pass off as your own.
  • Privacy in mind. Letters are drafted with blanks for account and identity numbers, so that you never type them into a chat.
  • Written for India. The examples use Indian situations, and many prompts ask for a result in Hindi or Punjabi as well as English.

Which assistant do they work with?

Any of them. The prompts are ordinary text with no special commands, so they work in whichever AI chat assistant you use, on a phone or a computer. Different assistants, and even the same assistant on different days, will give different answers to the same prompt. That is normal.

What a prompt cannot do

A good prompt improves the odds of a useful answer. It does not make the answer true. AI assistants can state wrong facts confidently, invent sources and misread your request. Use them for drafting, organising, explaining and practising. For facts, rules, fees and dates, go to an official source. For health, legal and money matters, go to a qualified person. The guide on checking AI answers gives a short routine.
